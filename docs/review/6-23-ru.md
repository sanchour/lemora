## BGN/PCGN 1947 (PCGN validity check, November 2022)

[Official table](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/636cd2828fa8f5357a0c652c/ROMANIZATION_OF_RUSSIAN_2022_final.pdf),
pages 1–3. Character map plus note 1/2 contextual e/ye and ë/yë; note 3 middle-dot distinctions;
note 5 case preservation. The code does not infer an unwritten ё. Stress marks are omitted from
the display conversion. Golden fixtures transcribed from the examples: Екатеринбург, Ёлкино,
Змеевка, Ульяновск, Лукъяновка, Щурово, Донское, Грозный. The synthetic note-3/case/boundary
regressions are identified separately in the test. Curly signs follow the table's U+2019/U+201D.

## ISO 9:1995, Russian subset

[Publisher preview of the standard](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/3589/3b8e97e9a40c4e668f22b30a0735fd4a/ISO-9-1995.pdf),
Table 1, pages 2–5 (Russian entries only). Golden fixtures from that table: муж, нижний,
общество, часы, щит, альбом, это, южный, яма. This is the modern Russian alphabet; the engine
refuses other Cyrillic letters instead of presenting a partially converted word. Not an assertion
of support for every Cyrillic language or historical Russian spelling.
